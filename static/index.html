Strategy Rules

4H Bias: Price within 50 pips of key level OR 150+ pips beyond BB (NAS100)

30m Confirm: Candle respecting VWAP / BB bands / EMA 9 + bullish/bearish close

5m Trigger: Break above/below 4-candle consolidation range

1m Entry: Candle close beyond breakout level → EXECUTE

Exit: TP1 (50%) at 30m middle BB · TP2 (50%) at 30m opposite BB or VWAP · SL: $20 fixed
